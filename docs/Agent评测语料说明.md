# Public Evaluation Corpus Candidates

The following are public university policy documents that can be uploaded as **external reference material** for RAG retrieval evaluation. They are not project rules and must be labeled with their original institution and source URL in the knowledge-base title.

1. Peking University, `公共教学楼内学术研讨空间预约管理办法（试行）`.
   - Covers booking in timetable-free slots, time-unit design and check-in expectations.
   - Source: https://dean.pku.edu.cn/userfiles/upload/download/202210061500263346.pdf
2. China University of Mining and Technology, `关于启用智慧自习教室的通知`.
   - Covers individual-seat booking, cancellation, Bluetooth check-in, no-show release and violation limits.
   - Source: https://gjzx.cumt.edu.cn/__local/5/12/41/55FD9D26D45F633D207692F1AC4_866CE0F9_BE451.pdf?e=.pdf
3. Nanchang University, classroom-management FAQ.
   - Covers open study-room allocation, booking, check-in and resource conflicts.
   - Source: https://video.ncu.edu.cn/docs/2024-03/368439ef35364dc7bcd86e94272d2e84.pdf

Suggested evaluation questions should be paraphrases rather than copied headings, for example: “迟到多久会被记违规？”、“课程空闲时段能否预约研讨教室？” and “取消后座位是否立即回收？”. Keep a source citation in the answer and do not mix external university policies with this system's own enforceable rules.
